> I joined **Roamless in February 2026 as a Senior iOS Engineer**, just as the team was preparing a major new release.
>
> This is the story so far — joining during a rebrand, helping the team adopt AI, automating delivery, introducing experimentation, and later becoming **App Squad Lead**. Throughout, the purpose has stayed the same: helping people stay connected.

## Joining Ahead of Roamless 2.0

When I arrived, the company was about to release **Roamless 2.0**. It brought **SMS and messaging within the app**, alongside a **rebrand and a visual refresh** of the experience.

It was a significant moment to join: the product was expanding the ways people could stay in touch, while the team was preparing to put a new identity in front of users. Roamless’s mission to help people stay connected gave that work a clear purpose, reaching beyond connectivity for travel alone.

## Bringing AI into the Engineering Workflow

After the release, I helped the team **introduce AI into its development workflow**. Alongside adopting the tools, I worked on making the app’s development setup suitable for that integration, with **safeguards to keep sensitive information out of reach of AI tools**.

For me, adopting a new tool means thinking about how it fits into the team’s everyday work and what it can access. Protecting sensitive information was part of making AI useful in practice.

## Moving from Manual Delivery to Xcode Cloud

The iOS delivery process was **entirely manual** when I joined. That made automation another clear opportunity to improve how we worked.

I introduced **Xcode Cloud for CI/CD**, helping automate the work involved in getting the app ready for delivery. It was a practical investment in the team’s foundations: making a repeated engineering task more consistent and reducing the manual effort around shipping.

## Creating Room for Experimentation

Alongside delivery automation, I introduced **feature flags and A/B testing** so the team could run product experiments.

This gave us a way to explore different approaches and learn from how people use the app. It connected the engineering work to a question that matters for the product: are the changes we make actually helping users?

## Becoming App Squad Lead

I was later **promoted to App Squad Lead**, expanding my role from iOS engineering to helping product and the **Android and iOS engineers** deliver together.

My focus now includes helping the squad deliver quality features that make it easier for users to find **the data roaming plan most suited to their needs**. That specific challenge sits within Roamless’s broader mission: helping people stay connected.

It brings together the parts of engineering I care about — understanding the user’s needs, working closely with product, and making pragmatic technical decisions that help the team deliver. The workflow improvements, automation, and experimentation all support that shared purpose.
